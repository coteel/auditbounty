Pay for bugs, not for noise. The triage happens in the contract.

A bounty pool pays per accepted finding, so every report needs a human to triage it first. AuditBounty moves that triage on-chain. The project funds a pool; an auditor files a finding with a stake behind it; an on-chain jury fetches the write-up and rules whether it is a real, actionable defect. Valid findings are paid and keep their stake — noise loses it into the pool.

How it works

  1. fund_bounty_pool (payable) — the project puts GEN in the pool that pays valid findings.
  2. submit_report (payable) — an auditor files a title, severity, write-up URL and expected bounty, and stakes GEN as custody.
  3. judge_report — the jury fetches the write-up on-chain (gl.nondet.web.get) and rules whether it names a component, states a failure mechanism and gives repro steps (gl.nondet.exec_prompt under consensus). One shot per finding.
  4. settle_report — exactly once: a valid finding is paid from the pool and keeps its stake; noise has the stake slashed into the pool.
  5. withdraw_pool — the admin takes slashed stakes and unspent bounty. No stake can be stranded.
